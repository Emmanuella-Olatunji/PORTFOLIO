BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things
you might ask.

What if I don’t have a complete brief?

You don’t need to have everything figured out. Tell me what you have in mind, and I can prepare a questionnaire tailored to your project to help us clarify the direction.

How much will my project cost?

Your quote depends on the scope, deliverables and timeline. Once we’ve discussed your brief, I’ll provide a quote for us to agree on before creative work begins.

Can I request changes?

Yes. You’ll have the opportunity to review the work and share feedback. We’ll agree on revision arrangements in advance; requests beyond the agreed scope may need an updated quote or timeline.

What files will I receive?

You’ll receive the final formats agreed for your project and its intended use, whether that’s social media, print or video. If you need editable source files, let me know when we discuss the brief so they can be included in the quote.
